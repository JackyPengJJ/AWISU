Introducing AWISU: autonomous warehouse inventory with a small unmanned aircraft.

The proposed platform combines a lightweight flight controller and onboard computing, within a three kilogram takeoff limit.

Side cameras read labels. Stereo and lidar support navigation. The rotating aircraft is a reference simulation asset.

First, aligned sensor observations build local geometry. Motion proceeds through a known-free corridor, with room to stop.
